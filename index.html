Misket Royale

Misket Royale is a free physics-based mobile marble battle game for Android. Aim, shoot, and knock rival marbles out of the arena to win.

About Misket Royale:

Welcome to Misket Royale! Join fast-paced, fun, and competitive real-time marble battles. Aim with your finger, shoot, and earn points by knocking your opponents out of the arena. Unlock special marbles, upgrade your trophies, and reach the top of the leaderboards!

User Data & Google Sign-In Purpose:

Misket Royale uses Google Sign-In solely to authenticate players, securely save game progress, and display display names on global score leaderboards. We do not access, share, or sell any personal user data for marketing purposes.
